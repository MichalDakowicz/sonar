# Update notes

## 2.0.0 — 2026-09-04

### Added
- Sonar is a native Android app, with the web version built from the same code
- Rate any album, owned or not — the score belongs to the release, not to your copy
- Ratings score production, vocals, lyrics and replay, with an overall you can drag
- Log a spin from any card and see the full listening history, day by day
- Collection can group by artist, year, genre, format or status
- Sort the shelf by shelf order, date added, artist, release, rating, last played or price
- Reorder your shelf by hand from Settings → Data
- Ratings is a tier list — search any album, drop it into a tier, no need to own it
- Stats cover formats, artists, eras, genres, stores, spending and how you rate
- "What should I put on?" draws a record from the shelf, or one you have been neglecting
- Friends' activity feed with reactions, plus a shareable public shelf
- Import a backup from the old app, or export everything as JSON

### Changed
- Rating an album no longer needs it on your shelf — scores stick to the release
- Sign in with the same account as Radar — one profile, one friend list, one privacy switch
- Profile visibility now covers your record shelf and your film shelf together
